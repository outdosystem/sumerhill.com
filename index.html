Welcome To Our Institution

A Symbol of Truth & Integrity in Education

Established in 1976 in a rented building near the old bus stand, Summer Hill Convent School today stands proudly in its own spacious double-storey campus on Mansa Road, Opposite Police Line, Bathinda.

Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, the school celebrated its historic Silver Jubilee in September 2001 and continues its noble mission of spreading literacy, character, and scholastic excellence across Punjab.

  • Holistic Junior Play-Way Method: Nurturing early curiosity with joy, activity, and experiential learning.
  • Inclusive Education: Special individualized care for weak and deserving students to develop them into responsible citizens.
  • Senior Secondary Wing: +1 and +2 classes in Medical and Non-Medical streams with modern laboratories.
  • Equal Opportunity: Commitment to quality education without any discrimination based on creed or background.

Management Leadership Message

Dedicated to the All-Round Development of Every Student

"Establishing Summer Hill Convent School was a pioneering attempt by our revered founder, S. Ajmer Singh Brar. We are dedicated to the all-round development of our students. Summer Hill Convent School is equipped with spacious classrooms, library, computer lab, science labs, and sports facilities.

As promised by us, we introduced +1 and +2 classes in our school. To educate better, we launched smart learning classes. Interested students are actively trained in the martial art of Wushu in the evening. The aim of our school is that each student should rise to excellence.

We believe that there is a dedicated teacher behind every good personality in the world. It is our teachers who are responsible for shaping our future: 'Those who educate children well are more to be honoured than parents; for these only give them life, those the art of living well.'"

Educational Philosophy

Teaching & Student-Centred Learning

Cultivating inquisitive, disciplined, and compassionate individuals through structured guidance and play-way exploration.

Play-Way Method for Juniors

In kindergarten and primary grades (LKG to 2nd), teaching is anchored in play-way methodologies, sensory games, storytelling, and hands-on discovery that eliminates fear of school.

Care for Deserving Students

Our philosophy reserves dedicated remedial support for students requiring additional academic guidance, ensuring no child is left behind regardless of initial aptitude.

Rigorous Senior Secondary Stream

For classes XI and XII in Medical and Non-Medical branches, curriculum delivery stresses conceptual depth, laboratory experiments, board examination preparation, and analytical rigor.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions regarding Summer Hill Convent School's affiliation, curriculum, classes, and facilities.

Summer Hill Convent School is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi under Affiliation No. 1630032 (School No. 20010). The school was first affiliated with CBSE in 1983 and holds State Government of Punjab NOC No. 8/12-80 Gr(1).

The school caters from early childhood (Nursery, LKG, UKG) through Senior Secondary (+1 and +2). In the Senior Secondary wing, we offer both Medical and Non-Medical streams with equipped science laboratories.

The school is located on Mansa Road, Opposite Police Line, Bathinda, Punjab – 151001. It operates in its own double-storey campus equipped with classrooms, playground, labs, and library.

Alongside traditional indoor games, dance, music, and annual sports days, Summer Hill Convent School offers specialized evening training in Wushu martial arts for physical agility and self-defense under trained supervision.

Parents can contact the school admission office directly via telephone (0164-5012613, +91 86994 58753, +91 94631 45404) or visit the campus on Mansa Road to collect the admission form and syllabus guidelines.
